Known receiver positions are rx_poses_m, in metres. IQ is indexed by position, TX×RX pair, then frequency. These are supplied model inputs, not estimated motion. Editing invalidates the old result. Apply checks both packets before replacing the current experiment.

Binding updates an identity hash only. It does not create new observations or certify coherence. A held packet may not reuse a training receiver configuration.

The inference contract

One ideal isotropic point, static scene, exactly supplied antenna positions and noise model. Independent unknown complex gains per pair/frequency are fitted on training positions only. The same gains predict held positions, with gain-estimation noise included. A finite grid and at most 48 bounded local refinements can miss other modes. Rank is a local diagnostic; it does not prove global uniqueness. Residual thresholds are heuristics, not confidence probabilities. No hardware capture, commodity WiFi calibration, multipath recovery, room scan, physical precision or body-pose claim.

The stable-slope example corrects a failure of a single snapshot. The exact changing-response example demonstrates why prediction alone cannot establish hardware stability. Reveal context only after inference to inspect the independently supplied simulation truth.
