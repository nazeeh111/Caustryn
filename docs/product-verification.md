# PhaseGlass RF product verification

The local Studio is a simulation and inference workbench. Its plotted coordinates and IQ traces are not evidence of a physical location, sensor performance or global uniqueness.

On 2026-11-16, a native browser exercised the local synthetic examples. In the single-snapshot mirror case, the fitted modes appeared at approximately y = ±1.145 m. The bounded receiver search proposed a modeled move; synthetic remeasurement left one found mode, with the finite-search qualification retained. At a 390 CSS-pixel viewport the responsive header exposed Model & limits, whose dialog opened with Enter and returned focus on Escape.

In the coherent sweep, the single-found-mode case declined to rank poses and explained that a finite search finding one mode does not prove uniqueness. The mirror example retained two modes, and its held-position predictions were evaluated without refitting or selecting a mode using held IQ. For two supplied new receiver configurations, the training-only planner ranked one as numerically distinguishable and the other indistinguishable under its roundoff gate. These are conditional results for the declared point model and stable per-link/frequency gains, not a statistical power or physical discrimination claim.

The held IQ plots were inspected in the local browser. They break traces at receiver-position boundaries and show a rounded amplitude scale; full numerical evidence remains available in the app and portable export. A portable coherent sweep ZIP was downloaded. The README single-snapshot image is an actual 1280 × 720 capture, and the planner image is an actual 390-pixel-wide capture of the two-pose training-only ranking from the current local synthetic session. The earlier coherent image is retained as historical material. Older recorded replay assets and released artifacts were not replaced.

The exact source's numerical, input and presentation checks are recorded with task evidence. The changed Python package uses development version `0.2.2.dev0`, distinct from the earlier `0.2.1` release. No real RF hardware, radio, sensor, field measurement or physical discrimination was tested.
